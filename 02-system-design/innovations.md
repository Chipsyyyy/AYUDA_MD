# Innovation Candidates and Their Status

## Current academic direction
Professors recommended investigating **candidate-to-job matching with explainable decision support**. AYUDA has expressed interest in matching but has **not yet confirmed a distinct DSS decision problem**.

| Idea | How it might help | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-opportunity decision support | Compare several suitable jobs/candidates with tradeoffs | Professor-suggested / team-proposed |
| Evidence-aware match explanations | Separate verified matches, confirmed gaps and unknown information | Team-proposed |
| Reciprocal preference-aware matching | Consider both job requirements and applicant preferences | Under research |
| Human-in-the-loop recommendations | Support rather than replace screening and hiring decisions | Design principle |
| Candidate profile freshness | Flag stale contact/availability information before rediscovery | Supporting enhancement |
| Skill and requirement normalization | Reconcile equivalent terms across resumes and vacancies | Supporting method |
| Rule-based follow-up priority | Highlight overdue or important pending recruitment actions | Supporting enhancement |

## Example of a defensible explanation
Technical support role: required troubleshooting skill **verified**; two years' experience **verified**; on-site availability **unknown**. Suggested action: recruiter confirms work arrangement and current availability. Do not report a fabricated probability of hiring.

## Innovation test
A feature is not novel merely because it uses AI, OCR, NLP, ranking or a Kanban board. Benchmark established capabilities, show the AYUDA-specific decision problem, justify the method and define measurable improvement. Final innovation remains open pending client answers and literature review.
