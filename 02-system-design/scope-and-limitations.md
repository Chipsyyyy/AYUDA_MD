# Scope and Limitations — Working Boundaries

## In scope for investigation
- Web and mobile components with distinct purposes.
- Candidate-side sourcing, rediscovery, reusable profiles and matching.
- Existing-client vacancy requirements as inputs to matching.
- Explainable DSS if AYUDA validates a decision need.
- Supporting résumé processing and recruitment monitoring.

## Not committed
- Direct applicant or client-company accounts.
- Exact matching model, ranking weights, explanation method, AI APIs or cloud services.
- Full client-specific endorsement automation.
- All staff augmentation/deployment processes.

## Excluded from current candidate-side direction
- Apollo-like prospective business lead discovery and contact scraping.
- Payroll, full HRIS and unrelated accounting.
- Automated hiring, rejection or guaranteed placements.
- Unrestricted external client access to talent records.

## Known limitations
Client company response time is not under AYUDA's control. Résumé parsing can be inaccurate; verification is necessary. Missing data must not be treated as confirmed mismatch. The system's final privacy/consent requirements and performance targets need validation.
