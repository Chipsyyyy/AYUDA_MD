# Preliminary MVP — Not Yet Approved

## Earlier MVP direction
Core candidate database, rediscovery, PDF/DOCX extraction, structured vacancies, candidate matching, workflow stages and reminders; applicant job discovery optional.

## Current focused MVP hypothesis
1. Verified reusable candidate profiles, including candidate rediscovery.
2. Structured job requirements for existing clients.
3. Explainable candidate-job matching with clear eligibility and unknown-data handling.
4. Human review and a minimal application/endorsement record.

This is a **planning hypothesis**, not the final MVP. A limited pipeline and résumé assistance may be required to demonstrate a complete workflow. Applicant/client portals, semantic models and full template automation are scope decisions awaiting validation.

## Success demonstration
A recruiter enters an active vacancy, finds a previously sourced candidate, reviews verified information, receives an explained recommendation, checks availability, and records a screening/endorsement decision without overwriting past recruitment history.
