# AYUDA Business Profile and Model

**Evidence status:** Working synthesis of AYUDA interviews and follow-up answers; confirm names, figures and location details against original documents before formal citation.

- **Organization:** AYUDA Business Management Solutions Inc.
- **Type:** Recruitment and talent acquisition firm; B2B recruitment services.
- **Headquarters:** Pasig City, Metro Manila. An extended office in Los Angeles was mentioned earlier; exact operational status remains unconfirmed.
- **Industries discussed:** BPO, IT/shared services, technology, hospitality and other corporate sectors.
- **Services discussed:** Direct hiring, executive search, candidate sourcing and recruitment coordination; certain staff augmentation/deployment arrangements involve sister company **ADORE PEOPLE SOLUTIONS INC.**

## Business model
Existing client companies provide hiring needs and evaluate endorsed candidates. AYUDA sources, screens, interviews, endorses and coordinates follow-ups. In direct hiring and executive search, the successful candidate generally becomes employed and paid by the client company. Staff augmentation has distinct contractual arrangements; do not assume AYUDA handles payroll through the proposed system.

## Stakeholders
| Stakeholder | Role |
|---|---|
| Talent Acquisition Lead | Oversight, assignments, candidate progress, client coordination |
| Recruiters | Source, screen, interview, endorse, follow up |
| Applicants | Submit qualifications, express preferences, participate in hiring |
| Existing client companies | Supply vacancies and requirements, conduct assessments, make hiring decisions |
