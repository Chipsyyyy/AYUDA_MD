# Academic Requirements and Project Context

**Status:** Academic context from team discussions; confirm final wording against official SYSAD rubric.

Team InterLink, FEU Institute of Technology, is taking System Analysis and Design. The group must identify **three professor-approved clients and corresponding title/system proposals** before the final title defense. AYUDA is one approved client; Plan International Philippines and Anytime Fitness Blue Residences are separate proposals and not part of AYUDA's scope.

For each proposal, the team must investigate the client's business model and processes, substantiate a real problem, propose web and mobile applications, justify a meaningful and feasible technical contribution, benchmark existing systems, review literature, and defend scope, architecture and evaluation.

## Developer responsibility at this stage
The developer must prepare **initial**, research-backed technology recommendations for each of six proposed modules: algorithm/model, libraries, frameworks, database, APIs, integration, hosting and feasibility. This is **title defense preparation**, not authorization to code.

## Paper and consultation considerations
The earlier initial client proposal used seven sections: (1) Business, (2) Business Profile, (3) Business Model, (4) Business Process, (5) Main Problem, (6) Proposed Solution, (7) Proposed Innovations. The later academic title proposal follows a separate instructor-provided template, including Area of Investigation and Background and Rationale. For the latter, introduce the client, explain current tools and processes, describe actual difficulties and justify why development is warranted.
