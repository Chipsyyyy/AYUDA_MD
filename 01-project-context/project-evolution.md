# System Concept Evolution

## Phase 1 — Applicant tracking
**Original title:** Development of a Web and Mobile Applicant Management System with Visual Workflow Monitoring for Recruitment Firms.

Planned centralized profiles, OCR/NLP résumé processing, Kanban pipeline, recruiter assignments, activity history, reminders and reports.

## Phase 2 — Sourcing and rediscovery
AYUDA's follow-up responses emphasized reusing existing candidate records and matching talent with vacancies.

**Revised working title:** Development of a Web and Mobile Candidate Sourcing and Recruitment Management System with NLP-Assisted Profiling and Explainable Job Matching for Recruitment Firms.

Six modules were articulated (see `../02-system-design/six-system-modules.md`).

## Phase 3 — Job matching and potential DSS
Two professor consultations recommended exploring **candidate-to-job matching and explainable decision support** as the primary technical focus. Standard ATS functions should support, not define, the innovation. Three possible perspectives: applicants, AYUDA recruiters and authorized client companies.

**Provisional title for consultation:** Development of a Web and Mobile Recruitment Decision Support System with Explainable Candidate-to-Job Matching for Recruitment Firms.

**Crucial:** Neither the DSS need nor three-sided user architecture is client-validated. The title, algorithms and scope are not final.
