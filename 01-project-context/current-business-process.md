# AYUDA's Current Recruitment Process

**Client-confirmed general stages** (not necessarily a strictly linear process):

1. **Sourcing:** Facebook, LinkedIn, job platforms, direct outreach, and existing records. Sourcing can precede a specific vacancy.
2. **Paper Screening:** Manual résumé review for qualifications, skills, experience and client requirements.
3. **Interviewing:** AYUDA preliminary screening/interviews.
4. **Endorsement:** Submit candidates to client companies; some require their own templates.
5. **Feedback:** Follow up on client assessments, interviews, status and next actions.
6. **Job Offer and Onboarding Assistance:** Coordinate offers and employment-start confirmation where applicable.

## Existing tools
Microsoft Excel, recruitment Gantt charts, email, messaging apps, AYUDA website, job/social platforms, and client systems when required. AYUDA said it had not adopted a dedicated ATS.

## Record design implication (proposed)
A candidate can be sourced once and considered for several vacancies. A **reusable master candidate profile** should be distinct from **individual applications/endorsements**, each with its own stages and outcomes. Rejection for one vacancy must not overwrite the candidate's master record.

## Important boundaries
AYUDA monitors recruitment but does not control all external client assessments or response times. Its existing website intake and external client-system integration require further validation.
