- Obtain sanitized Gantt chart, job descriptions, endorsement templates and candidate selection cases.

## DSS and workflows
- How are mandatory vs preferred requirements defined?
- Who compares candidates and approves endorsements?
- What decisions are difficult or time-consuming? How often?
- When one candidate fits multiple jobs, how is prioritization done?
- Does AYUDA want applicant recommendations and direct client-company accounts?
- Which candidate data may each role access?

## Technical
- Are there enough verified profiles and labeled recruiter judgments to evaluate matching?
- Are recommendations best explained by rules, multi-criteria scoring or model-based methods?
- Which web/mobile functions are essential for the MVP?
- What privacy, storage, hosting and testing constraints apply?
